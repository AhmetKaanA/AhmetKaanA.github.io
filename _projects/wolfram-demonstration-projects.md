## Overview

These Wolfram Demonstrations are interactive companions to my research on vibration control, boundary sensing, numerical filtering, and smart structures. Each presents a dynamic model that visitors can explore by changing material, initial-condition, damping, or feedback parameters.

## Demonstrations

Wolfram Demonstrations Project. Part of [Observability-Preserving Discretizations]({{ '/projects/observability-preserving-discretizations/' | relative_url }}).
