Professional Summary

Ph.D. Candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. My research focuses on numerical methods for partial differential equations, uncertainty quantification, low-rank scientific computing, stochastic Galerkin methods, computational modeling of collective cell migration, and control of distributed parameter systems.
