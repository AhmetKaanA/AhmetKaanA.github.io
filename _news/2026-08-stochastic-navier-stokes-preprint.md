Our preprint, [A Mean-Informed Low-Rank Monolithic Stochastic Galerkin Solver for the Unsteady Navier–Stokes Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07526), with Bedřich Sousedík is available on arXiv. It develops a low-rank solver for unsteady flow with uncertain viscosity. [Learn more about the research]({% link _projects/low-rank-stochastic-navier-stokes.md %}).
