## Course overview

This course combined advanced integration techniques with the study of sequences, infinite series, and multiple representations of curves. Reading quizzes and regular low-stakes assignments encouraged students to prepare before class and revisit difficult concepts throughout the semester.

This offering also provided the instructional setting for my Scholarship of Teaching and Learning work on students' interpretation of mathematical notation.

## Topics

- Techniques and applications of integration
- Improper integrals
- Sequences and infinite series
- Convergence tests
- Power, Taylor, and Maclaurin series
- Parametric curves
- Polar coordinates and polar calculus
