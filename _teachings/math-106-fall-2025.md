## Course overview

This course strengthened the algebraic foundations needed for later quantitative courses. It was designed especially for students returning to mathematics or seeking additional preparation before precalculus and calculus.

## Topics

- Linear equations and inequalities
- Polynomial and rational expressions
- Radicals and rational exponents
- Quadratic equations and applications
- Functions, domain, range, and graph transformations
- Inverse, exponential, and logarithmic functions
