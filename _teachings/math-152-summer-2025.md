## Course overview

This accelerated second course in calculus continued the study of integration and developed its geometric applications. The course then introduced infinite sequences and series, including convergence and representing functions through infinite series.

## Topics

- Techniques of integration
- Areas, volumes, arc length, and surface area
- Improper integrals
- Infinite sequences and series
- Convergence tests
- Power series and function representations
